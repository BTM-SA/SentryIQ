# SentryIQ
SentryIQ is a next-generation password manager and digital vault engineered to protect enterprise and personal credentials with uncompromising, military-grade security.

Built on a zero-knowledge architecture, the platform ensures your master password is never stored on disk and exists solely within your authenticated session. 

Your encrypted vault cannot be decrypted without your explicit authorization, guaranteeing complete data privacy.

🛡️ Ironclad Cryptography & Multi-Layered Defense At its core, SentryIQ utilizes advanced AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption to secure records before they ever touch physical disk space. 

To prevent undetected data modification, each vault save automatically generates a fresh, random initialization vector.
